## Abstract

A system-level analysis of narrative diffusion in social networks.

---

## 1. Problem Statement

Narratives propagate through networks with nonlinear amplification.

---

## 2. Model

We define influence as:

$$
I = \sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i \cdot r_i
$$

Where:
- \( w_i \) = trust weight  
- \( r_i \) = repetition frequency  

---

## 3. Hypothesis

Emotionally charged narratives maximize propagation velocity.

---

## 4. Results

Empirical simulations show:

- emotional signals > factual accuracy  
- repetition > originality  

---

## 5. Conclusion

Truth is not self-propagating. Structure determines survival.
